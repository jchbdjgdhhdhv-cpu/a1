Outfit Formulas

Stylists rarely start from scratch. They use a handful of proven combinations and change the colours, fabrics and shoes.

01

Why formulas work

A formula is a repeatable recipe: a set of shapes that balance each other. Learn a few and you can get dressed in minutes, every day, without looking the same.

02

Five everyday formulas

  1. Knit + straight trousers + loafers. Quietly smart.
  2. White shirt + dark jeans + trainers. The weekend classic.
  3. Blazer + T-shirt + tailored trousers. Office without stiffness.
  4. Slip dress + oversized knit + boots. Soft and grounded.
  5. Monochrome + one texture change. Effortless polish.

When everything shouts, nothing is heard.

03

Balance the proportions

Pair volume with something slim: a wide trouser with a fitted top, an oversized jacket with a narrow leg. Tucking in a top, even just at the front, shows the waist and keeps the outfit from looking boxy.

04

Let one thing lead

Choose one focus per outfit: a colour, a pattern, a statement shoe or a sculptural bag. When everything shouts, nothing is heard.

05

Dress for the day

Adjust the formula for the occasion with shoes and layers, not a whole new outfit. Try the outfit composer for ideas.
