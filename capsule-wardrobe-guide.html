The Capsule Wardrobe

A capsule is a small collection of clothes that all work together. Fewer decisions in the morning, more outfits from less.

01

Choose three colours

Pick one dark neutral (black, navy or charcoal), one light neutral (white, ivory or grey) and one accent you love. Everything in the capsule should fit this palette, so every piece goes with every other.

02

Start with the essentials

  • Two or three plain tops in your neutrals
  • One pair of well-fitting trousers or jeans
  • One skirt or second trousers
  • One jacket or blazer
  • One knit
  • Two pairs of shoes: one smart, one easy

Every piece should go with at least three others.

03

Fit beats everything

A small wardrobe only works if every piece fits. Have trousers hemmed and sleeves shortened if needed. A modest piece that fits perfectly looks better than an expensive one that does not.

04

Add texture, not quantity

Within a tight palette, texture keeps things interesting: wool next to silk, denim next to a crisp shirt, a leather belt against knitwear.

05

Review each season

At the start of each season, take out what you did not wear, note what was missing and add one or two pieces that fill real gaps. Use the capsule counter to see how much a single piece adds.
