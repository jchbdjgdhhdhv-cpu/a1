About the house

An independent house of style.

Outfits is an independent fashion guide about the art of putting clothes together.

We believe style is learnable. Our edits explain capsules, proportions and colour with the clarity of a good stylist, and our tools help you picture an outfit before you get dressed.

We do not sell clothes, take payment for mentions or use affiliate links. Every edit is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
